Billing FAQs

Straightforward Fee Policies

Zero surprise trip fees or hidden surcharges. Clear answers about payments and contingency timeline locks.

Why is pricing tiered by property square footage?
A 3,500 sq.ft home has double the branch circuits, plumbing fixtures, roof surface area, and HVAC tonnages of a 1,200 sq.ft condo. Tiered pricing ensures smaller properties don't overpay, while larger properties receive the full 3.5 to 4 hours of exhaustive evaluation.

Are there any extra travel fees or mileage surcharges?
Never within our standard service territory (45 miles from our metropolitan dispatch hubs). You pay the exact quoted package rate — no surprise gate fees, ladder charges, or rush dispatch penalties.

When do I pay for my home inspection?
Zero deposit is required to lock your contingency calendar date. Payment is settled securely online only after the on-site walkthrough is completed and before your final digital report and seller concession addendum are released.

What if the purchase falls through or the inspection is cancelled?
We understand contracts shift quickly. Simply notify our client care desk 24 hours prior to the scheduled time, and you can reschedule or cancel with zero cancellation fees.
