Scientific Inspection Protocol

How Our 3-to-4 Hour Physical Inspection Unfolds

We don't do 45-minute rush jobs. A standard InspectGuard residential home inspection is an exhaustive diagnostic examination where you are encouraged to walk along with your inspector.

01

Exterior Perimeter & Roof Exploration

Walk the perimeter, examine wall claddings, gutter discharge, roof flashings, foundation grading, and garage door reversal sensors.

02

Mechanical & Life-Safety Systems Testing

Remove electrical dead-front covers, fire up furnace and AC units, test water pressure and drain lines, and check smoke/CO detector dates.

03

Attic, Crawlspace & Infrared Thermography

Physically traverse attic rafters and subfloor crawlspaces to detect structural joist cuts, water stains, mold growth, and insulation gaps.

04

On-Site Verbal Debrief & Same-Day Digital PDF

Inspectors walk you through key defects on-site, followed by our full cloud-hosted report with high-res photos and repair urgency tags.

Interactive Digital Deliverable

Reports Engineered for Fast Decision-Making

No 80-page incomprehensible binders. Our digital inspection platform categorizes findings into standardized severity levels with clear next steps for contractors.

Priority 1

Critical Safety & Major Defects

Imminent electrical fire hazards, active gas leaks, structural support failures, or severe foundation compromises requiring immediate specialist repair.

Action: Immediate Contractor Quote

Priority 2

Repair & Maintenance Required

HVAC aging components, failing thermal window panes, minor plumbing drain leaks, and roof shingle granule deterioration that will worsen over time.

Action: Plan Near-Term Service

Priority 3

Homeowner Maintenance Tips

Filter replacement intervals, shutoff valve locations, gutter cleaning schedules, and general seasonal preservation recommendations.

Action: Ongoing Home Maintenance
