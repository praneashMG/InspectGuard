Client FAQ

Frequently Asked Booking Questions

Transparent answers so you know exactly what to expect from scheduling to closing.

How quickly can an inspector be on-site?
We maintain active emergency dispatch slots and can regularly accommodate inspections within 24 to 48 hours. If you have an urgent contingency deadline, call our direct line at (800) 555-7655 for immediate priority hold.

Do I need to attend the inspection in person?
While not strictly mandatory, we strongly encourage buyers to join the final 45 minutes of the inspection. The inspector will walk you through major shut-off valves, explain safety observations, and answer questions. If you cannot attend, we schedule a 1-on-1 phone or Zoom debrief.

How is entry arranged with the seller or listing agent?
Once you book, our dispatch team coordinates directly with the listing agent and title office to secure electronic lockbox (Supra/SentriLock) access. You don't have to spend your day chasing down keys or permissions.

When do I receive the completed digital report?
Every InspectGuard report is finalized and emailed same-day (typically within 12 to 18 hours of on-site inspection completion). You receive a secure digital portal link, downloadable high-res PDF, and our formatted 1-Page Concession Addendum.
