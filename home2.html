Buyer FAQs

Pre-Purchase Assessment Questions Answered

Everything you need to know about navigating property condition assessments before signing closing documents.

When in the home-buying timeline should I book this assessment?

+
Book immediately once your purchase agreement is accepted. Most standard contracts allocate a 7 to 10 day inspection contingency window. Booking within 24 hours of mutual acceptance gives you ample runway to digest findings, obtain specialized contractor bids, and negotiate repair credits.

How accurate are the itemized repair cost estimates in the report?

+
Our pricing models are benchmarked against live regional contractor and labor databases updated quarterly. While custom architectural or complex structural finishes can vary, our line-item estimates give your realtor an authoritative basis for realistic concession requests.

Can the seller see this pre-purchase assessment report?

+
No. The assessment belongs 100% to you as our client. You and your buyer's agent have total discretion over which excerpts, photos, or concession summary pages you choose to share with the seller during negotiation.

Do you inspect new construction before the builder walkthrough?

+
Yes, and it is strongly advised. New construction homes regularly suffer from rushed subcontractor work — uninsulated soffits, improperly wired GFCI circuits, disconnected ductwork, or missing attic baffles. Catching these prior to closing forces the builder to resolve them under warranty.
