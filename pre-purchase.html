WHY IT MATTERS

The average home has dozens of hidden issues. You only need to miss one.

A pre-purchase assessment is the only way to know what you're actually buying. It's not about finding reasons to walk away — it's about knowing exactly what you're walking into, what it will cost to fix, and what leverage you have at the negotiating table.

A Full Picture of the Property

Six core areas every pre-purchase assessment covers — photographed, evaluated, and rated for severity.

Structural Integrity

Foundation, walls, framing, and signs of settlement.

Electrical & Safety

Panels, wiring, outlets, and fire hazards.

Plumbing & Water

Supply lines, drains, heaters, and visible leaks.

HVAC & Ventilation

Heating, cooling, ductwork, and airflow performance.

Roof & Exterior

Shingles, flashing, gutters, and water drainage.

Attic & Insulation

Ventilation, moisture, and thermal performance.
